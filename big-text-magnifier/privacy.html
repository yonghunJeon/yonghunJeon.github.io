Big Text Magnifier Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Camera

The app uses the camera (CAMERA permission) only to show magnified text. Camera images are processed on-device in real time only; no photos or video are stored or transmitted.

2. Information the developer collects

The developer runs no server and does not directly collect personal information such as accounts, names, or contacts. Settings (zoom level, filter) are stored only on your device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention: IP address (used to estimate approximate location), app interactions (launches, taps), diagnostics (launch time, hang rate, etc.), and device identifiers (advertising ID, App set ID). This data is encrypted in transit. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.

4. Age

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over.
